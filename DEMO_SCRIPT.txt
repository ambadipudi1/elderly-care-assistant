[0:00 — HOOK]            
Elderly patients and their families often struggle to coordinate complex daily routines, from managing medication schedules to tracking physical and emotional well-being. Caregivers need to stay in the loop for critical changes, while giving the patient an easy, conversational way to manage their health.

[0:20 — WHAT IT IS]      
This is the Elderly Care Assistant, built for the Agents for Health and Social Good track. It uses a multi-agent ADK workflow to safely handle medication logs, well-being checks, and doctor visits while enforcing strict safety rules.

[0:40 — SHOW THE BANNER]

Our project delivers automated, secure, and intelligent care coordination right out of the box, wrapping complex medical tools behind a simple chat interface.

[1:00 — ARCHITECTURE]    
(show assets/architecture_diagram.png)
Let's look at the workflow. Every user request starts at the Security Checkpoint, which scrubs PII and checks for prompt injection or unsafe dosage requests. If it's safe, the Main Coordinator takes over and delegates to one of our specialized sub-agents: the Medication Manager or the Well-being Logger. These agents use our MCP Server to query real backend tools. If a critical change is requested, like a dosage update, the workflow pauses at the Human Caregiver Approval node before reaching the Final Output.
